Album sleeve notes — one title and a short back-cover blurb, 400 characters in total — for a jazz trio made entirely of frogs: tenor sax, upright bass and brushed drums, played straight as a working band.

## The entry

> Low Water, Late Set
>
> Three frogs, and no novelty act: tenor sax, upright bass, brushed drums, cut live in their low, crowded Tuesday room by the marsh. The saxophonist phrases long and breathy over a bass line that walks without hurrying; the drummer brushes time you can feel in your ribs. Standards, played close, with the silences left in.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The program writes the entry text to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`) and prints it.
